## Commute Connectivity: How to Reach Technopark from Prime City Hubs

Many newcomers worry that residing in central city hubs like Vellayambalam or Pattom will make their commute to Technopark difficult. In reality, Trivandrum boasts an efficient transit system:

### 1. KSRTC City Circular & Electric Swift AC Buses
The Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) operates a dedicated fleet of quiet, air-conditioned green electric buses running continuously along the **Central Station – Palayam – Pattom – Kesavadasapuram – Kazhakkoottam – Technopark** route.
- **Frequency:** A bus departs every 5 to 7 minutes during peak morning (08:00 AM – 10:30 AM) and evening (05:30 PM – 08:30 PM) hours.
- **Cost:** Fares range between ₹25 and ₹40 per trip, making monthly transit under ₹1,200.

### 2. NH-66 Bypass Transit via Two-Wheeler
If you ride a scooter or motorcycle, traveling from Pattom or Vellayambalam to Technopark Phase 1 or Phase 3 takes approximately 20 to 25 minutes via the wide, well-lit bypass highway.

### 3. Corporate Carpooling & Ride Sharing
With hundreds of tech professionals living in central residential hubs, ride-pooling via WhatsApp office groups and Quick Ride is exceptionally popular, cutting fuel costs while fostering friendships.
