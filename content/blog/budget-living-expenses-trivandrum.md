## Top 5 Money-Saving Lifehacks for Trivandrum Residents

Beyond accommodation, here are five proven tips to stretch your monthly budget in Thiruvananthapuram:

### 1. Master the KSRTC City Circular Electric Bus Network
Trivandrum was the first city in Kerala to implement the KSRTC City Circular electric bus system. These bright green low-floor AC buses run on color-coded circular loops (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Brown) connecting the Central Railway Station, East Fort, Palayam, Pattom, Vellayambalam, Medical College, and Technopark. 
- **The Hack:** A single ticket costs just ₹10. Avoid taking auto-rickshaws for short 2-to-3 kilometer trips, and you will save ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 every month.

### 2. Get a Membership at the State Central Library (Palayam)
Purchasing reference books, civil service test series compendiums, and competitive exam guides can cost thousands of rupees each term.
- **The Hack:** Join the State Central Library at Palayam. Membership fees are nominal (under ₹100 per year with a small refundable deposit), giving you access to tens of thousands of books, all national daily newspapers, and quiet heritage study halls.

### 3. Opt for Homestyle PG Food Over Food Delivery Apps
Ordering biryani, burgers, or rolls through Swiggy and Zomato easily costs ₹200 to ₹350 per meal. Doing this just four times a week adds over ₹4,000 to your monthly credit card or UPI bill.
- **The Hack:** Subscribe to the homestyle meal plan at Amanuro Stays or eat at local university messes. You will receive wholesome, balanced Kerala nutrition that keeps your body healthy and your wallet intact.

### 4. Use the KSRTC Student Concession Card
If you are an enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student at an affiliated college, apply for the official KSRTC Student Concession Card through your college office.
- **The Hack:** Student bus passes reduce your daily bus commute fares by up to 70% to 80% across ordinary KSRTC services.

### 5. Take Advantage of Free Public Recreation & Green Spaces
Trivandrum has exceptional, well-maintained public spaces where you can relax, exercise, and unwind without spending money:
- The Museum Gardens and Kanakakkunnu Palace grounds offer wide, green jogging paths and evening cultural events with free entry.
- Shanghumukham and Kovalam beaches are just a quick KSRTC bus ride away for weekend rejuvenation.
